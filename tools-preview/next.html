How we partner & invest

We are in the game of partnerships, so a long-term partner always has equity. There are three ways that happens.

Minority & majority shares

We use the four pillars and the optimisation framework to grow and streamline your business, and we take a share of it as your investment for that work.

Capital investment

If you need help and cash, or just cash, to grow the business, we are open to capital investment alongside the partnership.

Acquisitions

If you simply want out of your business, we are open to discussing a full acquisition and rolling it into the group.

The group

Every business we partner with joins the group, and businesses in the group work with each other at group rates. An event company and an AV company in the group get each other at a better price.
