2. Growth through optimisation

Ryan Tayler’s optimisation engine™

40Customers in the engine
Holding

Every business runs like an engine. Customers come in, you do the work, and the ones you impress come back or send someone new. The ones you don’t, leave.

Outside the cycle you’ve got your inputs. Paid marketing, SEO, organic. You pour them in to keep the engine turning over.

Two things decide whether it grows. How efficiently the cycle runs, how many customers you impress instead of losing, and how much input you add on top. When what you add is greater than what you lose, the business grows. When it’s less, it declines. That simple.

Adding 6 a month, losing 8.

Add more than you lose and the engine grows.

Here’s where most small businesses get stuck. They get the engine running at a decent efficiency, then quietly cut the inputs. Not enough team, not enough time, so they stop feeding it to hold a pace they can handle. The engine keeps turning at the same speed and growth flatlines.
