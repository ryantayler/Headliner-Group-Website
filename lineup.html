The advantage

Six businesses, one phone book

A staging company that needs vision on a Thursday, a promoter who needs trucks on a Sunday, a venue that needs a crew at four in the morning. Inside the group that's an internal call instead of three quotes and a gamble on someone you've never worked with.

  • Shared crew and gear across states
  • One set of insurances, one set of safety standards
  • Buying power on freight, hire and consumables
  • Work referred between the businesses at cost or close to it
Trucks, cases and crew moving between jobs. The group in motion.
Nobody is the best at all of it, so we stopped trying to be and bought the people who were.
