04 · OPERATIONS

Take the friction out of the machine

Processes, products, quoting, scheduling and handover. Usually the fastest money in the whole thing, because it is money the business already makes and loses again on the way out.

We get paid in equity

A consultant gets paid whether or not anything changes. We take a minority share in the business instead, so the only way this is worth anything to us is if it is worth more than it was when we found it. Capital investment and a full sale are both on the table, and the size of the share is per deal.

Nobody is the best at all of it

The instinct in this industry is to pull everything in house. Your own AV, your own crew, your own venue if the numbers ever stack up. It feels like control and it reads like margin, and it quietly puts a ceiling on the business, because nobody is the best at all of it.

We think the ceiling comes off when a business partners with people who are genuinely the best at their piece. You give up a clip of the ticket. You get back quality, reputation, and the time to be great at the thing you are actually great at.
