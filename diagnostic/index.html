Business Diagnostic

Fifty eight questions about the facts of your business. No opinions, no scoring, no grade at the end. It names the one thing capping you right now and tells you what to leave alone until that's fixed.

  • 01Roughly fifteen minutes. You can answer every question without opening your accounts.
  • 02Not sure is a real answer. Take it whenever it's true, because what you can't answer is a finding on its own.
  • 03You get one constraint. A twelve item to do list is the thing this is built to avoid, because the order you fix things in matters more than the list.
  • 04No AI is used anywhere in this tool, and none of your data is sent or stored offsite.

Answer it how it actually is, not how you'd like it to read.
