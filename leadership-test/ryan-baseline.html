Headliner Leadership Styles

Twenty eight questions, four axes, six styles. It does not stop at telling you what kind of leader you are. It tells you who to hire under you, and who you work best under.

The four axes

AxisScaleWhat it measuresRole
TriggerGut to ProofWhat you need before you act. Some move on a read. Some need it in hand first.Sets your style
DrivePeople, Systems, The workWhat moves the work forward. The people on it, the systems around it, or the work itself.Sets your style
MannerBlunt to MeasuredHow it lands. Blunt keeps the point intact. Measured protects the relationship.Screens the hire
ReactivenessImmediate to DeferredHow fast you get to it. Immediate hides nothing. Deferred arrives considered.Screens the hire

The six styles

StyleClosest readMade up ofIn one line
BelieverRichard BransonGut, through peopleSells the vision before it exists and pulls people in on belief alone.
ChargerElon MuskGut, through systemsBacks an instinct hard, then builds a machine around it at brutal speed.
PuristSteve JobsGut, through the workKnows what good looks like and will not ship until it is. Uncompromising on detail.
AnchorTed LassoProof, through peopleReads the evidence, then moves the org through trust rather than force.
OptimiserJeff BezosProof, through systemsMeasures everything and lets the numbers pick the direction. Builds lasting process.
ArchitectWarren BuffettProof, through the workWaits for the right thing, then commits and holds. Rare moves, huge conviction.

The rules behind the pairings

Trigger, always opposite. Someone has to be the one who says prove it, and someone has to be the one who moves before the proof arrives.
Drive, one step across, not two. Same drive and nobody covers the ground you skip. Two steps and you have no shared language.
Manner, opposite. Two blunt leaders and people stop bringing things forward. Two measured leaders and the point never lands hard enough.
Reactiveness, opposite. Two immediate leaders and every small thing becomes an event. Two deferred leaders and problems age while everyone plans.
No hierarchy. A match is a match whether you are hiring them or working for them.
