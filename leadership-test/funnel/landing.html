What it measures

Our test measures you on 4 scales. Each scale has 2 opposite ends that contradict each other, and each one measures a different way you think, act or make decisions in your business or professional life.

Trigger

GutProof

This measures how quickly you pull the trigger on a decision. You either act on your own gut or you act on proof.

Drive

PeopleSystemsWork

This measures how you drive success. Some people drive it by empowering people, some build systems, and others simply pursue the work itself.

Manner

BluntMeasured

This measures your manner in a professional capacity. Some people are more blunt and some are more measured. Neither is better than the other. Both have their own wins and downfalls.

Reactiveness

ImmediateDeferred

This measures how fast you react to problems. Some act immediately and do whatever it takes to deal with the problem. Others defer until they know for certain how it’s worth handling.
