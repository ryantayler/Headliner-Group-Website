Headliner Leadership Styles Test

Twenty eight statements about how you actually lead. It tells you which of six leadership styles you are, who to hire under you, and who you work best under.

Take the test → 28 statements. About five minutes.

What it measures

Every answer moves you along four scales. The first two decide your style. The other two decide who you should hire.

Trigger

Gut to proof

What you need before you act. Some move on a read. Some need it in hand first.

Manner

The six styles

Your style comes from two things. What you need before you act, gut or proof. And what moves the work forward, the people, the systems or the work itself.
