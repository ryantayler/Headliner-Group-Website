About

Back of house is where I learned the business

I'm an entrepreneur partnering with founders in live events and production. I buy into businesses in the industry, grow them through new ventures, partnerships and products, and I give founders the option to exit.

I've delivered more than 115 live events for over 56,000 attendees. Most of those hours were spent in the parts of an event the audience never sees. Load in at five in the morning, run sheets that change twice before doors, crew calls, freight, and the supplier who didn't turn up and the plan that replaced them by lunchtime.

Early in my career I worked for Aaron Sansoni, and that's where I learned how a business built around a stage actually runs. The events taught me the craft. The years around them taught me which businesses in this industry are worth backing and which ones only look like they are.

My goal is to be in business with, or partnered into, every area of live events and production in Australia.

the thing I actually believe

Partners, not suppliers

Most event businesses try to do everything themselves. Their own AV, their own crew, their own venue if they can swing it. It protects the margin and caps everything else, because nobody is the best at all of it.

I believe you grow faster by partnering with people who are the best at their piece. You give up a clip of the ticket. You get back quality, reputation, and the time to be great at the thing you're actually great at.

A supplier is someone you swap out when it's inconvenient. A partner is someone you stick with even when it is.

Ryan Tayler
